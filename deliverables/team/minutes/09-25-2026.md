### Project: Live SWOT Competitor Analysis Tool

- The industry partner currently tracks competitors manually via a spreadsheet; the goal is to automate this with AI agents
- **SWOT** = Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats — internal (S/W) vs. external (O/T) factors relative to the company
- The SWOT must update dynamically as competitors make moves; it is used by the sales team to position the company and answer investor/customer questions

### Key Features & Requirements

- **Central overview page** showing an aggregated SWOT graph/table across all competitors
- **Individual competitor pages** with a summary and drill-down details; clicking a data point reveals which competitor it relates to
- **Add competitor flow**: user inputs company name + website; system queues it for automated research and adds it to the overview
- **Automated research workflow** pulling from multiple sources: job postings, fundraising news, press releases, product updates, media
- **Key metrics to track**: automation level of competitors, new features, hiring trends (role types signal strategic direction), press releases
- **Refresh cadence**: runs once per week per company
- **Scoring**: features/updates can be scored (e.g. out of 10) for graphing and comparison
- **MVP scope**: start with manual competitor entry and basic research workflow; auto-discovery of competitors is a stretch goal
- **Target users**: founders and the sales team
- The tool should be **generic** (not compliance-specific) so it is broadly useful and résumé-worthy
